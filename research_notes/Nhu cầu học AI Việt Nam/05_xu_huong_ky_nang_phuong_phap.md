# Xu hướng kỹ năng AI ứng dụng & phương pháp đào tạo cho người lớn (2026), liên hệ người học Việt Nam

> Research date: 2026-10-31. Method: web search, using search-result summaries plus primary pages where they were reachable. Several Vietnamese news domains (vietnamnet.vn, tuoitre.vn) **could not be fetched in this session** because of network restrictions, so figures from them come from search snippets only and are marked [snippet].
> Caveat: AI tool rankings change every 3–6 months. Anything about tools below is a snapshot and needs checking again before a course launches.

## 1. Kỹ năng AI nào được nhà tuyển dụng và các báo cáo toàn cầu xếp hạng cao nhất (2025–2026)?

### Takeaway
All the major reports agree. "AI & big data" and "AI literacy" are the fastest-growing skills, and since 2026 AI literacy has turned into a **baseline** skill (the "new floor") rather than an advantage. The business value comes from **workflow redesign and agents** ("agent boss"), not from knowing how to use a single tool. Practical use is concentrated in writing, information seeking/practical guidance and tutoring/learning, which are the right "entry points" for non-coders.

### Cited Findings

Creative thinking, resilience/flexibility/agility, and curiosity & lifelong learning also rise. Secondary sources report net growth of 87/70/68 respectively (verify in the WEF data explorer) — [EU Digital Skills & Jobs Platform](https://digital-skills-jobs.europa.eu/en/latest/news/great-skills-reset-wefs-future-jobs-report-2025-catch-22-future-work); [WION](https://www.wionews.com/world/world-economic-forum-wef-report-78-million-new-jobs-added-by-2030-led-by-ai-big-data-and-cybersecurity-8602988); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1884646/global-job-market-poised-for-major-technological-shift-by-2030-wef)

### Inferences
- In Vietnam, consumer usage is already high (78% have tried AI), but much of it is personal/learning use. The market gap is in **turning casual use into reliable work skills** (workflow, data, automation), which matches the "high usage, few people trained" pattern in global reports.
- Learners use Vietnamese for nearly all prompts (89% on Gemini), so a course has to teach **prompting in Vietnamese** and test output quality in Vietnamese. It cannot simply translate English curricula.
- Ranking skill groups by **business impact × learnability for non-coders** (synthesized from the sources above; this is the author's judgment):
  1. AI literacy & safe use (low impact on its own, but mandatory; very easy to learn)
  2. Prompting/context engineering for writing, summarizing, translating, email, reports (high impact, easy; matches the 40% writing share of work use)
  3. Research & synthesis on your own documents (NotebookLM/deep research) (high, easy)
  4. Data/Excel/analysis with AI (high, medium)
  5. Marketing content: images, short video, voice (high for SMEs/sellers, medium)
  6. No-code automation (n8n/Make/Zapier) + chatbots for Zalo OA/Messenger (very high, medium–hard)
  7. Agents & "agent boss" (managing and delegating to agents) (very high, hard; trend for 2026–2027)
  8. Vibe-coding/no-code app building (high for some learners, hard)

### Gaps

## 2. Bức tranh công cụ 2026 và mức hỗ trợ tiếng Việt

### Takeaway
ChatGPT is far ahead in Vietnam (about 80%+ of chatbot share), with Gemini second (strong in education and Google Workspace). Microsoft 365 Copilot officially supports Vietnamese for text, but voice is limited. For Vietnamese voice, local providers (Vbee, FPT.AI) still have the edge on regional accents, while ElevenLabs only supports Vietnamese on some models. Detailed vendor evidence on Vietnamese support for image/video/automation tools is **sparse**.

### Cited Findings
- **Market share/usage in Vietnam:** ChatGPT about 82.6% and Gemini about 9.0% of mobile chatbot share (06/2026) — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share/mobile/viet-nam). ChatGPT, Gemini, then AI Hay lead in MAU (H1/2026) — [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/vietnam-innovation/vietnamese-users-clock-700-million-hours-on-ai-apps-5105781.html). Note the difference: StatCounter measures traffic share, Sensor Tower measures MAU, and Decision Lab measures "have used" (ChatGPT 81%, Gemini 51%, Meta AI 36%) — [Decision Lab](https://www.decisionlab.co/blog/vietnam-consumer-ai-market-2025-78-of-online-population-have-engaged-with-ai-one-third-turn-it-into-a-daily-habit)
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot:** Vietnamese was among the 12 languages added in 2025, bringing the total to 42 supported languages — [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/microsoft-365-copilot-officially-adds-support-for-12-more-languages/); [Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/microsoft-extends-copilot-language-support-to-include-12-new-languages/). - **Vietnamese voice/TTS:**

### Inferences
- **Proposed core course stack (snapshot 10/2026, to be rechecked):**
  - Chat assistant: ChatGPT as the main tool (most learners already use it), Gemini as the second tool (free, tied to Google Workspace/Android, strong in Vietnamese). Mention Claude/Copilot for office users on M365.
  - Research: NotebookLM (grounded on your own documents, which reduces hallucination) plus deep-research features.
  - Visual content: Canva AI (familiar to Vietnamese SMEs and marketers) plus a native image model (GPT image/Gemini image). Video: CapCut (very popular for TikTok/Reels) plus one generative video model (Veo/Kling).
  - Voice: Vbee/FPT.AI for natural Vietnamese voices, ElevenLabs for international/multilingual work.
  - Automation: n8n (self-hostable, cheap, a fit for Vietnamese SMEs) or Make. Zapier is easier but costs more.
  - Vietnam-specific channels: chatbots for Zalo OA and Facebook Messenger are the most practical "agent" application for Vietnamese SMEs, but **no vendor sources were found in this round**.
- Because tools change quickly, a curriculum should be designed around **skills/workflows** (e.g. "draft → verify → finalize", "automate a lead-capture flow") with tools as interchangeable modules, updated every quarter.

## 3. Trình tự học hợp lý (prerequisites) và các khung năng lực AI

### Takeaway
International frameworks (UNESCO 2024, OECD/EC 2025, EU AI Act Art. 4) all put **understanding + ethics/safety** first, then **using/co-creating**, then **managing/deploying**, and finally **designing/building**. This supports the sequence: AI literacy & safe use → prompting/context → content → data → automation → agents → building apps. Vietnam already has its own AI Law (in force 01/03/2026), and a course should include a module on it.

### Cited Findings
- **UNESCO AI competency frameworks (09/2024, Digital Learning Week):**
  - Students: 4 dimensions (human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI techniques & applications, AI system design) with 3 progression levels, reported as Understand → Apply → Create (labels from secondary sources) — [UNESCO NZ](https://unesco.org.nz/knowledge-hub/unesco-ai-competency-framework-for-students-and-teachers); [Teachnet](https://teachnet.ie/unesco-ai-teacher-and-student-competency-frameworks/)
  - Teachers: 15 competencies, 5 dimensions (human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI foundations & applications, AI pedagogy, AI for professional learning), 3 levels Acquire → Deepen → Create — [RPTU fdtech](https://fdtech.cs.rptu.de/en/wissenschaftstransfer/ki-kompetenzrahmen-lehrerinnen); [García Vallejo Substack](https://maricruzgarciavallejo.substack.com/p/unescos-ai-competency-frameworks)
- **OECD & EC "Empowering Learners for the Age of AI" – AI Literacy Framework** (review draft 05/2025, final version expected in 2026): 4 domains, **Engaging with AI → Creating with AI → Managing AI → Designing AI**, with 22 competences, linked to the PISA 2029 Media & AI Literacy assessment — [BABL AI](https://babl.ai/oecd-and-european-commission-unveil-draft-ai-literacy-framework-for-schools/); [AVID Open Access](https://avidopenaccess.org/resource/an-ai-literacy-framework/); [Computing at School](https://www.computingatschool.org.uk/resources/2025/august/an-ai-literacy-framework-for-primary-and-secondary-education/)
- **EU AI Act Article 4 (AI literacy):** applies from 02/02/2025 to providers and deployers. The Commission's Q&A (07/05/2025) sets no fixed standard; the required level depends on the AI system's risk and the employee's role. Even staff who use chatbots for ad copy or translation must be informed of risks such as hallucination. An internal training record is enough, and no external certificate is required. National authorities start enforcement from 08/2026 (2 or 3/8/2026 depending on the source) — [European Commission AI Literacy Q&A](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers); [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/european-commission-publishes-q-a-on-ai-literacy); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2025/05/eu-ai-act-update-ai-literacy-questions-and-answers). (Secondary sources mention a Digital Omnibus amendment to Art. 4; unverified.)
- **Vietnam's Law on Artificial Intelligence No. 134/2025/QH15:** passed 10/12/2025 (429/434 votes), in force **01/03/2026**, 8 chapters and 35 articles, risk-based management, regulatory sandboxes, a national AI fund. Systems already in operation get transition periods (about 18 months for healthcare/education/finance per one source). It prohibits using AI to impersonate people for fraud or manipulation — [VnEconomy](https://en.vneconomy.vn/national-assembly-approves-vietnams-first-ai-law.htm); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/02/vietnam-artificial-intelligence-law-foundation-and-outlook); [Lao Động](https://news.laodong.vn/thoi-su/nghiem-cam-dung-ai-gia-mao-nguoi-de-lua-doi-thao-tung-co-chu-dich-1623285.ldo); [Vietcetera](https://vietcetera.com/amp/en/vietnam-is-the-first-south-east-asian-country-to-pass-an-ai-law)
- Microsoft WTI 2025: "agent boss" (directing and managing agents) is presented as a new role for everyone, the step after individual use — [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/2025/04/24/the-2025-annual-work-trend-index-the-frontier-firm-is-born/)

### Inferences
- Proposed **Vietnamese roadmap** (mapped to the OECD/EC domains):
  - Level 0 – AI literacy & safe use (Engaging + ethics; Vietnam's AI Law, personal data, deepfakes)
  - Level 1 – Prompting & context in Vietnamese; verifying output (Engaging/Creating)
  - Level 2 – Content: writing, images, short video, voice (Creating)
  - Level 3 – Data/Excel/Sheets with AI; research on your own documents (Creating/Managing)
  - Level 4 – No-code automation + Zalo/Messenger chatbots (Managing)
  - Level 5 – Agents / agent boss (Managing)
  - Level 6 – Vibe-coding / building AI apps (Designing)
- Levels 0–1 are prerequisites for everyone. Levels 2/3 can run in parallel by role (marketing vs office/finance). Levels 4–6 need comfort with data structure and logic (Level 3).
- EU Art. 4 is not binding in Vietnam, but it is a useful benchmark for **B2B corporate training** (especially for FDI firms or companies exporting to the EU): a short "AI literacy" course with a training record.

### Gaps
- Could not access the **Google AI Essentials / Microsoft AI Skills** curricula directly to compare module order (not searched, due to the call limit).
- No official text was found on whether the Vietnamese AI Law has provisions on **AI literacy/training** for businesses; the implementing decrees need checking.
- No empirical evidence was found (beyond the frameworks) showing that learning in this sequence produces better outcomes; the sequence rests on expert consensus.

## 4. Phương pháp sư phạm: cohort vs self-paced, học qua dự án, dữ liệu công việc thật, coaching — và yếu tố tạo ROI

### Takeaway
The strongest evidence: (a) **enough training (≥5 hours), in person or with a coach**, goes with more regular AI use (BCG 2025); (b) AI helps beginners/lower-skilled workers most (14–34%) and helps a lot on tasks "inside the frontier", but **lowers accuracy on tasks outside the frontier** (−19 to −23 points). So a course has to teach learners to *recognize where AI is strong and weak*, not just to "use it". Cohort-based learning completes clearly better than self-paced, but most of the data comes from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- **BCG AI at Work 2025** (26/06/2025): 79% of employees with more than 5 hours of training are regular users, vs 67% of those with less. Regular use is clearly higher with access to **in-person training and coaching**. Only just over a third say they received adequate training. 18% of regular users have had no training. Frontline use is stuck at 51% — [BCG press release](https://www.bcg.com/press/26june2025-beyond-ai-adoption-full-potential); [UNLEASH](https://www.unleash.ai/artificial-intelligence/bcgs-ai-at-work-2025-report-four-takeaways-for-hr-leaders/); [BigDATAwire](https://www.bigdatawire.com/this-just-in/bcg-report-companies-must-go-beyond-ai-adoption-to-realize-its-full-potential). BCG also stresses that value comes from **workflow redesign**, not just deployment — [TechEdgeAI](https://techedgeai.com/bcg-report-reveals-real-ai-gains-come-from-workflow-redesign-not-just-tool-adoption/)
- **Dell'Acqua et al. (Harvard/BCG, "Jagged Technological Frontier", WP 2023; published in Organization Science):** 758 BCG consultants, GPT-4 (2023). On tasks *inside the frontier*: +12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, quality about 40% higher. On tasks *outside the frontier*: AI users were 19 points less likely to be correct (some sources say about 23% worse), and the AI-assisted arguments were *more persuasive even when wrong*. One condition got a prompt-engineering overview — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/10/06/generative-ai-chatgpt-collaboration-bcg/); [AI-wiki summary](https://businessdatasolutions.github.io/ai-wiki/sources/2026-04-28-dellacqua-jagged-technological-frontier). (Data from 2023, GPT-4.)
- **Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, "Generative AI at Work"** (NBER WP 31161, 2023; QJE 140(2), 2025): 5,179 customer support agents. Issues resolved per hour rose 14% on average, and **34% for novice/low-skilled workers**, with little effect on experienced workers. Customer sentiment and retention improved, and there are signs that workers learn from the AI — [NBER](https://nber.org/papers/w31161); [SIEPR](https://siepr.stanford.edu/publications/working-paper/generative-ai-work)
- **Noy & Zhang, Science (2023)**: 453 professionals doing writing tasks. ChatGPT cut time by about 40% and raised quality by about 18%. Most of the time saved came from faster first drafts; accuracy was not checked — [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/995024); [TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2023-07-productivity-effects-chatgpt-college-educated-professionals.amp)
- **Cohort vs self-paced:**
  - An MITx Supply Chain study: switching a course from instructor-paced to self-paced cut completion by 6% and pass rate by 10% (the most rigorous comparison found, since only pacing changed) — [Ruzuku summary](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/cohort-vs-self-paced-completion-study)
  - Ruzuku (2026, about 27,000 courses): cohorts 53.4% vs self-paced 41.9% completion (another Ruzuku page gives 64.2% vs 48.2%, which is inconsistent). MOOC industry median 12.6% (Class Central 2023) — [Ruzuku](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/cohort-vs-self-paced-completion-study); [Ruzuku language benchmarks](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/course-success-index/language-teaching)
  - Disco (vendor): cohort 85–95% vs MOOC 3–15%, with no published method — [Disco](https://www.disco.co/learning-community). Caution: selection bias (people who choose cohorts are more motivated).
- **Anthropic Economic Index 03/2026 "Learning curves"**: describes users improving their effectiveness over time with experience — [startuphub.ai](https://startuphub.ai/news/claude-usage-diversifies-learning-curves-emerge) (secondary).

### Inferences
- Recommended design for Vietnam:
  1. **Cohort-based/live (online or in person) with a minimum of 5–10 hours**, plus a follow-up coach or community (Zalo/Facebook group) for 2–4 weeks after the course. This matches BCG's evidence on hours, in-person learning and coaching.
  2. **Learn by doing on learners' own work data and tasks** (bring-your-own-task), because gains are concentrated where the task matches the AI's capabilities, and learners have to discover their own "frontier".
  3. **An explicit "jagged frontier" module**: hands-on exercises where AI gets things wrong in a convincing way, to train verification habits.
  4. **Self-paced microlearning only as a supplement** (videos and prompt libraries for review), not as the main format, given its low completion rates.
  5. Market positioning: the biggest gains go to beginners and lower-skilled workers, so **practical courses for non-coders/office workers/SMEs** have a strong ROI story.
- ROI comes more from **redesigning workflows** (McKinsey ~3×, BCG) than from teaching tools on their own, so a capstone project should be "redesign one real process" with before/after time measured.

### Gaps
- No primary **Section (Section School) AI Proficiency Report** data was found (not searched).
- No studies on **GitHub Copilot** productivity were collected in this round (well known: a 2022/2023 RCT found about 55% faster task completion, but no source was gathered here, so it should not be cited without checking).
- No evidence specific to Vietnamese learners (e.g. completion rates for Vietnamese online courses, or a preference for online vs offline) was found.
- No rigorous study on microlearning specifically for AI skills was found.

## 5. Rủi ro cần dạy: hallucination, dữ liệu cá nhân, bản quyền, deepfake, đạo đức

### Takeaway
At minimum, a course must cover: hallucination/overtrust (the evidence on "persuasive but wrong"), personal and corporate data, deepfakes/impersonation (now prohibited by Vietnam's AI Law), and copyright of AI output. The best practice is to **embed** risks in each practical exercise rather than leaving them for a separate theory session.

### Cited Findings
- Outside the frontier, GPT-4 users were less accurate, and AI produced arguments that were more coherent and persuasive *even when the answer was wrong*, which is the core risk to teach — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/10/06/generative-ai-chatgpt-collaboration-bcg/); [AI-wiki](https://businessdatasolutions.github.io/ai-wiki/sources/2026-04-28-dellacqua-jagged-technological-frontier)
- Noy & Zhang did not check the accuracy of AI-assisted output, a reminder that speed/quality gains do not guarantee correctness — [TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2023-07-productivity-effects-chatgpt-college-educated-professionals.amp)
- EU AI Act Q&A: even staff who use chatbots for ad copy or translation must be informed of specific risks, "for example hallucination"; the training level depends on the risk and the role — [EC Q&A](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers); [Burges Salmon](https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102kazh/ai-literacy-european-commission-publishes-faq)
- Vietnam's AI Law strictly prohibits using AI to impersonate people in order to deceive or manipulate, and applies risk-based management. Draft fines were up to 2 billion VND (organizations) / 1 billion VND (individuals) (final text to be verified) — [Lao Động](https://news.laodong.vn/thoi-su/nghiem-cam-dung-ai-gia-mao-nguoi-de-lua-doi-thao-tung-co-chu-dich-1623285.ldo); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/new-technology/1754898/vietnam-the-first-law-on-artificial-intelligence-what-you-must-know)
- UNESCO and OECD/EC put "ethics of AI" and "human-centred mindset" as core dimensions alongside skills, not as an add-on — [UNESCO NZ](https://unesco.org.nz/knowledge-hub/unesco-ai-competency-framework-for-students-and-teachers); [BABL AI](https://babl.ai/oecd-and-european-commission-unveil-draft-ai-literacy-framework-for-schools/)
- Stanford AI Index 2026 notes a widening public **trust gap**, a reason to teach responsible use — [Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report)

### Inferences
- Embedding approach: every module ends with a "safety checklist" (verify sources and figures; don't paste customer data or national ID/CCCD numbers into free tools; label AI content; check rights to images/voices/likenesses; never clone a voice or face without consent).
- In Vietnam, also teach Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection and the Personal Data Protection Law (2025). No source was gathered for these in this round, so treat this as a suggestion to verify.

### Gaps
- No sources were gathered on **copyright of AI output under Vietnamese law** (Intellectual Property Law), or on the PDPL/Decree 13 in relation to AI training. This needs separate research.
- No data was gathered on deepfake/scam statistics in Vietnam.

## 6. Đo lường kết quả học tập cho khóa AI

### Takeaway
The credible metrics in the studies are **task time (time saved), output quality as rated by an independent evaluator, accuracy, and frequency of regular use after the course**. A practical course should combine a pre/post test, a timed before/after task on real work, a portfolio/capstone, and a 30–60 day usage-frequency survey.

### Cited Findings
- Noy & Zhang measured **task completion time** (−40%) and **quality rated by blinded evaluators** (+18%), a design that can be copied for pre/post exercises — [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/995024)
- Dell'Acqua et al. measured **tasks completed, speed, quality, and accuracy** separately, showing that a single "speed" metric can hide accuracy losses — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/10/06/generative-ai-chatgpt-collaboration-bcg/)
- Brynjolfsson et al. used a business **output metric** (issues resolved per hour) plus customer sentiment and retention — [NBER](https://nber.org/papers/w31161)
- BCG uses the share of **"regular users"** (frequent use) as the adoption metric, linked to training hours and format — [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/press/26june2025-beyond-ai-adoption-full-potential)
- EU AI Act Q&A: an **internal training record** is enough to show compliance; no certificate is required — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2025/05/eu-ai-act-update-ai-literacy-questions-and-answers)
- McKinsey measures value through **EBIT attributable to AI** (≥5% for high performers), the organization-level metric — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)

### Inferences
- A 4-level measurement framework (adapted from Kirkpatrick; the Kirkpatrick source was not gathered in this round):
  1. Satisfaction/NPS
  2. Pre/post knowledge test (including spotting hallucinations, safety situations)
  3. Behavior: share of learners using AI ≥3 times a week after 30/60 days (the BCG "regular user" metric)
  4. Results: time saved on 1–3 real tasks (timed before/after), output quality rated by managers/peers, and the number of workflows automated (portfolio/capstone)
- For B2B: produce an "AI literacy training record" per employee, as in the EU Art. 4 guidance, as a value-added service.

### Gaps
- No published standard AI proficiency test was found (e.g. the Section AI Proficiency score, the Microsoft/Google certificates) to use as a pre/post benchmark.
- No data on real time savings for Vietnamese learners after AI courses was found.
